CEGURADISTA: SMART STRUCTURAL CONSULTATION PLATFORM 
Company: CivilPH 
Website: CEguradista 
Overview: 
This solution is a web-based platform that connects homeowners and small contractors with licensed 
Civil/Structural Engineers in their area. Its main goal is to make professional structural consultation more 
accessible, affordable, and convenient. 
Key Features: 
1. Engineer Directory with VerificationCo 
○ List of licensed Civil/Structural Engineers 
○ PRC license internal verification 
○ Location-based filtering (city/region) 
○ Specializations (residential design, retrofit, inspection, etc.) 
○ Experience and portfolio preview 
This ensures homeowners avoid unqualified practitioners. 
2. Online Appointment Booking System (Core Feature) 
Homeowners can: 
○ View engineer availability (calendar view) 
○ Choose consultation type: 
■ On-site structural inspection 
■ Online consultation 
■ Design review 
■ Quotation request 
○ Select date and time 
○ Receive automated confirmation via email/SMS 
○ Get reminders before the appointment 
This reduces delays and removes the difficulty of finding engineers manually. 
3. Transparent Service Packages 
○ Fixed consultation rates (basic inspection, full structural assessment, etc.) 
○ Estimated turnaround time 
○ Clear service inclusions 
○ Review and rating system 
○ Report feature 
This helps homeowners feel more confident about cost. 
4. Payment Options 
○ Cash 
○ GCash 
○ Card 
○ QRPH 
5. Digital Reports & Recommendations 
After inspection: 
○ Engineer uploads structural assessment report 
○ Repair or retrofit recommendations 
○ Estimated repair cost range 
This ensures proper documentation and professional accountability.
